Why You Use Your Own Free Google Gemini API Key

Auto Job does not use a shared developer key. Every user brings their own personal, 100% free key from Google AI Studio. Here is why this is essential:

🆓

Google Gives It Away for Free

Google AI Studio provides every Gmail account with 15 requests per minute and 1,000,000 tokens daily at zero cost. No credit card is required.

⚡

Zero Rate Limit Collisions

If hundreds of users shared Meeran's single API key, Google would throttle or block the key immediately. Using your own key gives you a dedicated, unthrottled line.

🔒

Complete Privacy & Isolation

Your API key is saved exclusively in your local data_folder/secrets.yaml file. It is never transmitted to any external server or saved in Git.

Get Your Free Key in 60 Seconds:

1
Go to Google AI Studio

Visit aistudio.google.com/app/apikey and log in with any Gmail account.

2
Click "Create API Key"

Click the blue button and choose "Create API key in new project". Copy your generated key.

3
Paste in Auto Job GUI

Open Auto Job's Keys tab (or in the Colab form) and paste your key. You are ready to apply infinitely!

Core Features

Built to Handle Complex Job Portals

Standard autofill extensions break on dynamic career pages. Auto Job uses multi-engine browser automation to navigate and submit.

🎯

Greenhouse, Lever & Ashby Support

Automatically detects target ATS software, handles nested iframes, populates contact details, and triggers the submission buttons.

📊

Excel Formula & Hyperlink Parser

Extracts URLs from Excel =HYPERLINK() formulas, handles company redirect trackers, and cleans application links automatically.

📎

Automated Resume Attachment

Locates file inputs across various layouts and attaches your PDF resume without browser security warnings.

🤖

AI-Powered Questionnaire Filling

Answers custom application questions (e.g. "Why do you want to join?", "Years of React experience") using your own free Gemini key.

🛡️

Safe Pacing & Account Protection

Does not touch LinkedIn automation by default, keeping your professional social accounts 100% safe from flagging.

📑

Executive Audit Reports

Generates detailed APPLICATIONS_REPORT.md and success.json files containing timestamps and direct confirmation links.
